## Proposal 2 (Progressive Reminder Alert Stack, `B3.3.50-4`) — implemented, DONE

### 2. Dedicated Progressive Reminder Alert Stack (`B3.3.50-4`) Replacing XUL `<notification>`

Firefox's XUL `<notification>` bar (`gBrowser.getNotificationBox()`) forces buttons into a single non-wrapping horizontal row, which is why a 5-button calendar reminder clips on the right. Replacing XUL `<notification>` with a Zentral top-layer (`popover="manual"`) reminder alert stack solves three problems at once:

1. **Zero Button Clipping:** Actions wrap cleanly into two compact rows (`Open` · `Done` · `Dismiss` on row 1; `Snooze 10m` · `Snooze 1h` · `Tomorrow` on row 2).
2. **True 3-Stage Visual Escalation:**
   - **Stage 1 (`pre`):** Compact, translucent corner toast (`~320px`) with a gentle accent border and auto-dismiss or quiet acknowledgment.
   - **Stage 2 (`due`):** Elevated card with stronger surface contrast, countdown/due badge, and full action row.
   - **Stage 3 (`followup` — unanswered after Stages 1 & 2):** High-prominence centered/top-edge card with a larger footprint, distinct high-contrast border/surface tint, and a slow, smooth CSS color shift (`3s ease-in-out infinite alternate` between two calm accent tones, disabled under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` — strictly avoiding rapid flashing/strobing for photosensitive users).
3. **Cross-Window Dismissal:** Because `createReminderHistoryStore` will emit change events (`B3.3.50-3`), clicking **Done**, **Snooze**, or **Dismiss** in one window immediately closes the alert card in all other open Zen windows.
